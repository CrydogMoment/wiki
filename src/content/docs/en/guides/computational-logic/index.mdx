Binary is the foundation of every computer's operation, be it a mobile phone, an ECU in a car, or the largest supercomputer in the world. 

On its own, it can look quite confusing as a seemingly random collection of ones and zeroes, but to a computer it is just like reading plain text.

## What is binary? 

Binary is just another way of representing a number. While we represent numbers using a combination of 0-9, binary only has the numbers 0 and 1.
